45 patients with stage III NSCLC underwent NAI in this cohort. Paired tumor biopsies, surgical tissues, serial plasma and Peripheral blood mononuclear cell (PBMC) samples were analyzed via multi-omic approaches, including DNA/RNA sequencing, cfDNA profiling and transcriptomic sequencing. SigMA was applied to infer mutational processes, and RNA-seq deconvolution combined with multiplex immunofluorescence was used to characterize the tumor microenvironment.
